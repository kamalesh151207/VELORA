Demo Policies & Disclaimer

🎓 Academic Project

VELORA is a college main project front-end application. It operates entirely client-side in the browser. No real products, real money, or real orders are involved.

💰 Pricing Rules

  • GST: Demonstrative 18% flat rate on taxable amount.
  • Delivery: Free for orders ≥ ₹999. Otherwise ₹79 flat fee.
  • Coupons: WELCOME10 (10% off, min ₹1000, max ₹500), SAVE100 (₹100 off, min ₹799), FREESHIP (free delivery).

🛡️ Privacy

All data (cart, wishlist, orders) is stored only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to any external server.
